# Unit 5 — Past Perfect

## Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:
- Understand when to use the Past Perfect tense
- Form affirmative, negative and interrogative sentences
- Distinguish between Past Simple and Past Perfect

## Grammar Focus

### Form

| | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I/You/We/They | had + past participle | hadn't + past participle | Had ... + past participle? |
| He/She/It | had + past participle | hadn't + past participle | Had ... + past participle? |

### Usage

We use the Past Perfect to talk about an action that happened **before** another past action.

> When I arrived at the station, the train **had already left**.

## Exercise 1 — Complete the gaps

Complete the sentences with the Past Perfect form of the verb in brackets.

1. By the time we got to the cinema, the film __________ (start).
2. She __________ (never / travel) abroad before her trip to London.
3. They were tired because they __________ (not / sleep) well.
4. __________ he __________ (finish) his homework before dinner?
5. I couldn't get in because I __________ (forget) my keys.

## Exercise 2 — Past Simple or Past Perfect?

Choose the correct form.

1. When I __________ (arrive), they __________ (already / eat).
2. She __________ (call) me after she __________ (read) my message.
3. We __________ (not / know) each other before we __________ (meet) at the party.
