# Vocabulary — Animals

## Domestic Animals

| English | French | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| dog | chien | My dog loves to play fetch. |
| cat | chat | The cat is sleeping on the sofa. |
| rabbit | lapin | She has a white rabbit. |
| hamster | hamster | His hamster runs on a wheel. |
| goldfish | poisson rouge | We have three goldfish. |

## Wild Animals

| English | French | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| bear | ours | Bears hibernate in winter. |
| wolf | loup | The wolf howled at the moon. |
| eagle | aigle | An eagle flew over the mountain. |
| deer | cerf | We saw a deer in the forest. |
| fox | renard | The fox is very cunning. |
